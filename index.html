On the Horizon

Principles

Boundaries that every tool respects, without exception.

🔒

Local-First & Zero Cloud

There are no servers: your thoughts, recordings and memories stay on your device. No tracking, no telemetry, no ads.

🎙️

Human Integrity Above AI

No voice cloning, no biometric harvesting, no synthetic replacements. A real human voice is treated as an heirloom.

⏳

40+ Year Digital Longevity

Open, universal standards (Ogg Opus, SQLite, self-contained HTML), so what you export can still be opened decades from now, even if this project disappears.

🍂

Artistic Nostalgia

Visual and melodic tributes inspired by works that explore impermanence and time (Plastic Memories, Clannad, Violet Evergarden, Frieren).

The Soundscape of Evanescent Echo

Every feature, line of code, and color scheme was created while listening to contemplative melodies about love, loss, and the passage of time.
